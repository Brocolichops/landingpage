Our story

Aux Pictures was founded in 2026 by Richard Aires, Essa Alavi, and David Ortega. We met at the beginning of 2025 in college through a film club and collaborated on multiple short films. Apart from our shared interest in films, we also have a shared love for music and decided to come together to form Aux Pictures, a production company focused on music videos and related content.

We understand the importance of providing visual representation for music in today's digital age. Not only through music videos, but also through clips for social media, teasers, and promotional posts. Our goal is to help artists enhance their music's reach and impact through compelling visuals that resonate with their audience.

Based in New Jersey.

How a project runs

This is the default workflow (we adapt per project).

  • Consultation — goals, vibe, references, scope.
  • Plan — locations, schedule, shot list, talent needs.
  • Shoot — efficient coverage, strong direction, clean data.
  • First cut — fast turnaround so you can react early.
  • Revisions — notes in rounds so we move forward, not sideways.
  • Final delivery — exports ready to post.
