Privacy Policy

Short version: we don’t run ads, we don’t sell data, and we keep things simple.

Last updated: 2026-01-11

What we collect

This site does not have accounts and does not directly collect personal information through a backend form.

Email you send us

If you email us, we receive whatever you include (name, contact info, project details, links, etc.). We use it only to respond and coordinate your project.

Estimate builder (localStorage)

The estimate builder stores your selections in your browser using localStorage so your choices persist between pages. This data stays on your device unless you choose to send it to us (for example, by emailing).

Hosting + logs

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical data (such as IP addresses) for security, abuse prevention, and site reliability.

(GitHub notes that visitor IP addresses may be collected for security purposes.)

Cookies

We do not intentionally set tracking cookies on this site. If you click links to third-party sites (like YouTube or Instagram), those sites may use cookies or tracking under their own policies.

Third-party links

This site contains links to external platforms (YouTube, Instagram, etc.). We’re not responsible for their privacy practices. Check their privacy policies if you care about what they track (and you probably should).

How we use info

  • To respond to booking requests and questions
  • To plan and deliver services you request
  • To communicate scheduling, pricing, deliverables, and revisions

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We only share information if needed to deliver your project (for example: a vendor we hire for your shoot) or if legally required.

Your choices

You can reset the estimate builder at any time (it clears localStorage selections). You can also contact us and ask what information we have from your emails.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Head to the contact page.

No weird stuff.

We’re here to make videos, not to build a surveillance economy.
